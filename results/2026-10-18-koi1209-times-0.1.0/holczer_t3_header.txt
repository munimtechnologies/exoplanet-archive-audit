      96 I1     ---     Over    Overlap flag (5)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Note (1):
    * = we could not derive an uncertainty thus we used the median of the
        uncertainties instead.
Note (2): equal to the (duration of transit - average)/average. A 9.999e+00
          indicates a NaN value.
Note (3): A 9.999e+00 indicates a fixed duration and depth.
Note (4): The sum of:
    0 = not an outlier, 
    1 = no-variation p-value > 0.025, 
    2 = local TTV, 
    4 = global TTV, 
    8 = global TDV, 
   16 = global TPV, 
   32 = TTV, TDV or TPV uncertainty outlier (see Section 2.4).
Note (5): 
    0 = no overlapping, 
    1 = another relatively large interfering transit (>0.25 the "area" of the 
        transit) too close (expected time difference < 2 transit durations + 1 
        interfering transit duration).
