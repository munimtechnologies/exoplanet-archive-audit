# Pre-scoring note: Lissauer et al. 2024 Table 1 keeps KOI-4777.01 at 0.824 d, although their text halves it

## What we checked by hand

Lissauer et al. 2024 (PSJ 5, 152), Section 2.3, say they halved two candidates' periods after checking
signals that Caceres et al. (2019) list at small integer multiples or fractions of DR25-supplemental periods: KOI-6262.01 "from P = 0.673 to 0.3365 days" and KOI-4777.01 (found with
the correct period by Cañas et al. 2022) from 0.824 to 0.412 days. They also changed two false
positives: KOI-4305.01 (0.935 to 0.234 d) and KOI-4872.01 (1.035 to 0.207 d).

Their machine-readable Table 1
(https://content.cld.iop.org/journals/2632-3338/5/6/152/revision3/psjad0e6et1_mrt.txt), read on 2026-10-17,
against the Archive's cumulative KOI table read the same day:

| KOI | KIC | Lissauer Table 1 period (d) | Status | Archive cumulative period (d) | parm_prov | Lissauer / Archive |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6262.01 | 2283362 | 0.33657302 | PPNP | 0.673141323 (CANDIDATE) | q1_q17_dr24_koi | 0.500004 (1/2) |
| 4305.01 | 5536715 | 0.23377317 | FFNN | 0.935086665 (FALSE POSITIVE) | q1_q16_koi | 0.250002 (1/4) |
| 4872.01 | 11496490 | 0.20705385 | FFNN | 1.035268597 (FALSE POSITIVE) | q1_q16_koi | 0.200000 (1/5) |
| 4777.01 (Kepler-1971 b) | 6592335 | **0.82400164** | PPNP | 0.824000125 (CONFIRMED) | q1_q17_dr24_koi | 1.000002 (1/1) |

Three of the four corrections in Section 2.3 are in the table. KOI-4777.01's row is not: it lists
0.82400164 +- 0.00000205 d, the old period, with an uncertainty that suggests a fit at that period, not a
typing slip.

The Archive's planet table already has the right value. Its default row for KOI-4777.01 (host KOI-4777,
alias Kepler-1971) gives 0.412 d from Cañas et al. 2022 (2022AJ....163....3C); its four non-default Kepler
project rows (Q1-Q12, Q1-Q16, DR24, DR25 supplemental) give 0.824000125 d. The cumulative KOI table keeps
the pipeline value, as it does for other KOIs.

## What this means

- **For our pre-registration:** prediction 9 said K04777.01 and K06262.01 would come out at ratio 1/2 and
  K01353.03 at 1/5. K06262.01 should hold. K04777.01 should come out at 1/1 and will not be period-flagged,
  so that part of prediction 9 will miss. The miss is ours: we took the period change from the paper's
  text without reading the table row. We will score it as a miss, not adjust it.
- **For Lissauer et al. 2024:** the published Table 1 row for KOI-4777.01 seems not to carry the period
  correction their Section 2.3 describes. The arXiv listing (2311.00238, v1 2023-11-01, v2 2024-09-10)
  shows no changelog, and we found no erratum. Anyone using Table 1 alone for KOI-4777.01 gets the 0.824 d
  alias. This is a small inconsistency in a published table, not a new result about the planet: the 0.412 d
  period is Cañas et al. 2022's. We have not contacted the authors.
- **For the Archive:** nothing new to report here. Its default planet row is right; the KOI table is a
  pipeline product.

## Limits

- Periods and Status were read by WebFetch from the 7 MB fixed-width file, one row at a time, and
  character positions were counted by hand. The lissauer-xmatch-v1 job reads the whole file by byte range
  and will confirm or contradict these four rows.
- We read only the HTML text of the paper (Section 2.3), not the PDF, and did not check the arXiv v2 text.
- If a later IOP revision of the table exists beyond "revision3", we have not found it.
